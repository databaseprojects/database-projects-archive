databaseprojects

Database Projects is a set of conceptual photo series that run unofficial censuses of the small parts of everyday life that go unseen because they're ordinary, like car colours, bricks, and empty storefronts. Each one is shown as a series, counted and filed, so the pattern becomes the picture.

A quiet index of photographs and records. The filing line above is the way through.
