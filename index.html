databaseprojects

Database Projects is a set of conceptual photo series collecting everyday scenarios, objects, and situations, like car colours, bricks, and empty storefronts. Each one is shown as a series, counted and filed, so the pattern becomes the picture.

A quiet index of photographs and records. The filing line above is the way through.
